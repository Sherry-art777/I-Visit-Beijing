About the Project

I Visit Beijing is an AI-driven one-stop service platform designed for overseas tourists visiting Beijing. It solves core pain points such as language barriers, information asymmetry, cultural adaptation, and inconvenient travel experiences. Based on the collaboration of domestic and international large models, the platform provides whole-process, multi-language, and personalized intelligent tourism services, helping global visitors enjoy a better travel experience in Beijing.

Core Services

AI Personalized Itinerary Planning

Generate one-stop solutions covering food, accommodation, transportation, tours, shopping and entertainment.

Multi-language Real-time Interaction

Support voice, text and photo translation for barrier-free communication in Beijing.

AI Crowd & Attraction Prediction

Forecast passenger flow and recommend off-peak routes to avoid crowds.

Cultural Adaptation Guidance

Provide etiquette tips, attraction rules, consumption protection and emergency assistance.

One-stop Booking & Price Comparison

Hotels, tickets, restaurants and homestays can be reserved in one click.

AI Travel Log Generation

Automatically generate travel videos and multilingual travelogues.
